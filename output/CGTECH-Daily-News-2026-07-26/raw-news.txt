ARTICLE 3: AI-Powered Sleep Analysis for Early Alzheimer's Diagnosis
Source: Medical Xpress
URL: https://medicalxpress.com/news/2026-07-ai-analysis-early-diagnosis-alzheimer.html

SUMMARY:
Researchers from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid have developed a methodology using AI to analyze brain electrical activity during sleep, facilitating early diagnosis of Alzheimer's. The study demonstrates that analyzing nocturnal brain waves with machine learning techniques can noninvasively identify early neural alterations. This breakthrough could lead to more effective treatment and care for Alzheimer's patients. The methodology classifies patients into three distinct biological subgroups, enabling personalized treatment approaches. Early diagnosis is crucial for improving the quality of life for Alzheimer's patients.
